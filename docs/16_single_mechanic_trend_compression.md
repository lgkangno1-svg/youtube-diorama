# 16 — Single-Mechanic Trend Compression

Updated: 2026-08-24

## Why this changes the current plan

The previous TK-004 gummy concept asked Veo to keep three different translucent gummy textures visually distinct across multiple shots. That is attractive in theory but expensive in practice: three material states create more chances for texture drift, duplication, shape changes, and rerolls.

Fresh Japan-market evidence on 2026-08-24 points to a simpler trend mechanic: a newly launched national gummy product centers its play value on **slowly lifting a long gummy without breaking it**. Separately, an August 2026 youth survey ranked gummy products first among items Japanese high-school students tend to grab first in convenience stores.

We should copy neither the product nor its packaging. We should copy only the content mechanic:

> **one object + one visible rule + one progressive physical risk + one proof ending**

This is better suited to Tiny Cat Kitchen because it combines current Japanese youth relevance with a lower-generation-risk Flow task.

## Current trend translation

TK-004 is now:

- 3mm generic micro gummy
- tiny ruler visible from the opening frame
- cat slowly stretches one continuous strand
- strand nearly snaps before a visible goal mark
- cat slows down and crosses the mark
- final proof is the same unbroken gummy laid beside the ruler

Do not use:

- brand names
- package designs
- branded straw/powder preparation
- exact commercial product shape
- exact flavor pairing

## Cost/quality principle

When two concepts have similar audience upside, prefer the concept with the **lower visual-state entropy**.

Practical ranking for Veo exploration:

1. one object / one motion / one measurable goal
2. one object / one transformation
3. two-object comparison
4. multi-material comparison
5. multi-character action with many object-state changes

Higher-entropy concepts require more continuity control and therefore have a higher expected reroll cost even when the nominal generation price is unchanged.

## Trend Gate v2

A trend may interrupt the CORE queue only if all five conditions pass:

1. **Fresh Japan evidence:** ideally within 14 days.
2. **Target fit:** evidence specifically relevant to teens/20s or a clear youth platform signal.
3. **Immediate visual rule:** understood without audio within ~0.5 seconds.
4. **Original translation:** no brand/package/character copying.
5. **Low state entropy:** can be expressed with one dominant object and one conflict when possible.

If condition 5 fails, keep the trend as an idea but do not spend Flow credits until a simpler translation is found.

This ordering preserves IP introduction while exploiting the current trend before it decays.

## Loop Engineering rule

For every future trend idea, ask twice:

- **First pass:** what is popular?
- **Second pass:** what is the simplest physical mechanic that expresses why it is popular?

Only the second-pass mechanic should reach Flow.

The goal is not to maximize prompt complexity. The goal is to maximize **retention signal per generation**.
