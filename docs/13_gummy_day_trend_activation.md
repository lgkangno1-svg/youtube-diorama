# 13 — Gummy Day Trend Activation (2026-08-24)

## Why this is worth interrupting the normal queue

Japan has a short, unusually strong teen/20s confectionery window before **September 3, Gummy Day (グミの日)**.

Fresh signals checked on 2026-08-24:

- FamilyMart announced five limited/early-release texture-led gummies beginning **2026-08-25**, emphasizing chilled-shari-shari, crispy+bouncy, melt-in-the-mouth, and mochi-like textures.
- Seven-Eleven Japan launched seven limited gummies around the same cultural moment, emphasizing visual novelty and unusual texture.
- A recent Japanese high-school convenience-store survey reported gummies as a leading impulse-grab category.

This is useful for Tiny Cat Kitchen because the trend is not just a flavor trend. It is a **texture transformation trend**, which maps directly to macro miniature video.

## Do not copy products

Do not reproduce retailer packaging, logos, brand names, exact product shapes, or exact product/flavor combinations.

Benchmark only the structural signal:

> Japanese teens currently have a timely reason to care about unusual gummy texture.

Translate it into our IP:

> **3 mm gummy + three texture methods + cat as tiny food scientist.**

## New trend gate

A trend episode may interrupt the CORE queue only when all four are true:

1. Japanese relevance is evidenced by a current source within 14 days.
2. The trend has a clear visual mechanic, not merely a keyword.
3. Tiny Cat Kitchen can transform it without copying a brand/product.
4. The episode introduces a new fingerprint or world-state change.

If any criterion fails, keep the CORE schedule.

## Post-publish decision

At 24h:

- High Stayed-to-watch + weak APV → tighten texture comparison section only.
- Weak Stayed-to-watch + high APV → replace OPEN/hook only; do not reroll G2–G4.
- Weak both → stop spending credits on the gummy hypothesis.

At 72h:

- Strong subscriber conversion → keep the `tiny food scientist / candy lab` world-state and reuse it later with a different mechanic.
- Strong reach but weak subscriber conversion → keep as seasonal reach asset, not a CORE format.
- Strong both → schedule one follow-up **after** Gummy Day using a new sensory mechanic, not another three-gummy comparison.
